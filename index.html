In English

Satıcı Radar is a Chrome extension for sellers on Trendyol and Hepsiburada, Turkey's largest marketplaces. It adds price history, competitor seller prices, sales estimates and price-drop alerts to product pages. The free plan includes 10 product analyses per day. Pro costs 149 TRY/month with a 7-day free trial and unlocks unlimited analyses, competitor prices, price alerts and CSV export. Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy; after purchase the customer receives a license key by email to activate Pro in the extension.
